KARNATAKA VEHICLE REGISTRATION POLICY

NEW VEHICLE REGISTRATION REQUIREMENTS IN KARNATAKA

DOCUMENT REQUIREMENTS

For registering a new vehicle in Karnataka, the following documents are required:

1. Form 20 (Application for registration of a motor vehicle)
2. Sales Certificate (Form 21) from the dealer
3. Manufacturer's Certificate (Form 22)
4. Valid Insurance Certificate
5. Address Proof (any one of the following):
   - Aadhaar Card
   - Voter ID Card
   - Passport
   - Ration Card
   - Utility Bill (Electricity/Water/Telephone) not older than 3 months
   - Rental Agreement (if residing in rented accommodation)

6. Age Proof (for first-time vehicle owners):
   - Birth Certificate
   - School Leaving Certificate
   - Passport
   - Aadhaar Card

7. Passport Size Photographs (2 recent photographs)
8. Pollution Under Control (PUC) Certificate
9. Temporary Registration Certificate (if applicable)

PROCEDURE AND TIMELINE

- Temporary registration is valid for 1 month from the date of purchase
- Permanent registration must be completed within 30 days of purchase
- The registration process typically takes 7-10 working days
- Vehicle inspection is mandatory for all new registrations

FEES

- Registration fee: ₹600 for two-wheelers, ₹1000 for four-wheelers
- Smart card fee: ₹200
- Road tax: 2% of vehicle cost for two-wheelers, 3% for four-wheelers
- Late fee: ₹100 per month if registered after 30 days

SPECIAL REQUIREMENTS

- For commercial vehicles, additional permits are required
- For transport vehicles, fitness certificate is mandatory
- For imported vehicles, customs clearance certificate is required
- For electric vehicles, special green registration is available with reduced road tax

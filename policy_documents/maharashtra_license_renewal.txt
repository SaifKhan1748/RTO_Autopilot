MAHARASHTRA LICENSE RENEWAL POLICY

DOCUMENT REQUIREMENTS FOR LICENSE RENEWAL IN MAHARASHTRA

For renewing a driving license in Maharashtra, the following documents are mandatory:

1. Original Driving License (Expired or about to expire)
2. Form 9 (Application for renewal of driving license)
3. Address Proof (any one of the following):
   - Aadhaar Card
   - Voter ID Card
   - Passport
   - Ration Card
   - Utility Bill (Electricity/Water/Telephone) not older than 6 months
   - Bank Passbook with current address
   - Rent Agreement (if residing in rented accommodation)

4. Age Proof (if license expired more than 3 years ago):
   - Birth Certificate
   - School Leaving Certificate
   - Passport
   - Aadhaar Card

5. Passport Size Photographs (3 recent photographs with white background)
6. Medical Certificate (Form 1A) for applicants above 40 years of age

PROCEDURE AND TIMELINE

- The application must be submitted at least 60 days before the license expires
- If the license has already expired, it can be renewed within 3 years from the date of expiry
- If expired more than 3 years, a fresh license test is required
- The renewal process typically takes 10-20 working days
- Online appointment booking is mandatory for all renewal applications

FEES

- Renewal fee: ₹250 for two-wheelers, ₹350 for four-wheelers
- Late fee: ₹75 per year if renewed after expiry
- Online service charges: ₹50
- Smart card fee: ₹150

SPECIAL CASES

- For commercial vehicle licenses, additional fitness certificate from authorized center is required
- For transport vehicle licenses, a medical certificate from a government-approved doctor is mandatory
- For applicants with disabilities, special medical certification from a government hospital is required
- For senior citizens above 65 years, additional medical fitness test is required

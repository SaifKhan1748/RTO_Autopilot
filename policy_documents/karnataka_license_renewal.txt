KARNATAKA LICENSE RENEWAL POLICY

DOCUMENT REQUIREMENTS FOR LICENSE RENEWAL IN KARNATAKA

For renewing a driving license in Karnataka, the following documents are mandatory:

1. Original Driving License (Expired or about to expire)
2. Form 9 (Application for renewal of driving license)
3. Address Proof (any one of the following):
   - Aadhaar Card
   - Voter ID Card
   - Passport
   - Ration Card
   - Utility Bill (Electricity/Water/Telephone) not older than 3 months
   - Bank Passbook with current address

4. Age Proof (if license expired more than 5 years ago):
   - Birth Certificate
   - School Leaving Certificate
   - Passport
   - Aadhaar Card

5. Passport Size Photographs (2 recent photographs)

6. Medical Certificate (Form 1A) for applicants above 50 years of age

PROCEDURE AND TIMELINE

- The application must be submitted at least 30 days before the license expires
- If the license has already expired, it can be renewed within 5 years from the date of expiry
- If expired more than 5 years, a fresh license test is required
- The renewal process typically takes 7-15 working days
- A learner's license may be issued if the renewal is not processed within the grace period

FEES

- Renewal fee: ₹200 for two-wheelers, ₹300 for four-wheelers
- Late fee: ₹50 per year if renewed after expiry
- Additional service charges may apply

SPECIAL CASES

- For commercial vehicle licenses, additional fitness certificate is required
- For transport vehicle licenses, a medical certificate from a government-approved doctor is mandatory
- For applicants with disabilities, special medical certification from a government hospital is required
